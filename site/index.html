fair questions

Do I still have to print receipts?

Where the law requires a printed fiscal receipt, you keep printing it — ditto sits beside your printer, not instead of it. It replaces everything else you hand over: e-receipt copies, tickets, warranties, menus, return slips.

What if the wi-fi is down?

Your till gets an immediate "box offline" answer. Nothing shows, nothing queues, nothing is charged.

What if it can't show within a minute?

The request expires and the held credit is released. A late code never appears — nobody sees a stranger's receipt.

Shown but never scanned — do I still pay?

Yes. The credit pays for the showing; scanning is free whether zero people scan or fifty.

Do I need a developer?

If your till or POS can call a URL when a sale closes, it's one request. If it can't, whoever set up your POS can usually add it in an afternoon.

How many boxes do I need?

One per counter where you hand things to customers. They share one balance and one console.

What does it cost?

One credit per code actually shown. We're setting pack prices together with pilot customers — pilots start with 50 credits on the house.

What does it show between codes?

Whatever you design: your logo, colours, opening hours, the time. Every screen is a canvas in the console.
