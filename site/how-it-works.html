paper, replaced

A small screen, instead of a piece of paper.

Receipts, tickets, order slips — printed, handed over, lost. ditto is a palm-sized touch screen that sits by your till. Where you would have printed, it shows a square code instead. The customer points their phone camera at it, and the page opens right there — theirs to keep.

Nothing to install — not for you, not for them.

step by step

So what actually happens?

Four things, in order, every single time. Scroll the list — the map on the right follows. The small grey lines are for your developer; feel free to skip them.

01

Your till has something for the customer

A receipt, a ticket, a booking. Your system sends it our way — one message, carrying a link to the page you already host. We never see what's on that page.

POST /api/v1/devices/dev_8f21/trigger · { action:"show_qr", payload:{ url } } · Idempotency-Key
02

We check, and set a credit aside

Is the request really from you? Is that box switched on? Then one credit goes on hold. On hold — not charged.

api key · scope devices:trigger · device: online · credit: 1 reserved
03

The code appears — that's when you pay

About a second later, the exact box you named shows the square code and confirms it. That confirmation spends the held credit — one credit, only for a code actually shown. Box off, moment passed? Nothing shows, nothing is charged.

mqtt d/dev_8f21/cmd · ack { ok:true } · credit: settled · late trigger → expired, released
04

The customer scans it

They point their camera — the page opens in their phone's browser. No app to download, no account to create, no email address to spell out loud.

GET <your url> — their phone, their browser
