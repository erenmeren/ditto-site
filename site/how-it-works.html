Receipts, tickets, order slips — printed, handed over, lost. This box sits by your till; where you would have printed, it shows a square code. Their camera does the rest. Four things happen, in order, every single time — scroll the list, the map follows.

A receipt, a ticket, a booking. Your system sends it our way — one message, carrying a link to the page you already host. We never see what's on that page.

02

A couple of seconds later, the exact box you named shows the square code and confirms it. That confirmation spends the held credit. Box off, or a minute gone by? Nothing shows, nothing is charged.
